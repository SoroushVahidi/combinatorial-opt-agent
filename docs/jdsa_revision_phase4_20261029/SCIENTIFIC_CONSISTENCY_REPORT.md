## Interpretation and terminology

Schema retrieval is target-template identification in an exposed, benchmark-derived catalog. Coarse readiness checks coverage/types; Strict additionally requires schema match. Complete scalar agreement additionally checks every required reference scalar within the stored inclusive tolerance. Conditional Exact20 averages eligible schema-hit query fractions and must not be interpreted as whole-query success. For ratio-aware TF–IDF, 7 ≤ 255 ≤ 265 on the same 331-query frame is a logical nesting of these predicates, not autonomous-system accuracy.

The gold-key eligibility and reference-assisted parameter inventory remain explicit in the definitions, Algorithm 1 and Figure 1. Matched grounding variants change candidate construction, context, scoring, abstention and decoding together. Their observed 7/331 to 46/331 difference is not an isolated causal effect of search. Frozen-score controls support narrower slot-level observations, with no net full-instance gain on their separate 49-query population.

Structural output validity, execution, terminal-objective agreement, numerical extraction, model-label agreement and human semantic correctness remain different endpoints. The common-18 denominators 15 and 16 are not combined into a leaderboard. PaMOP's precision-limited aggregate remains withheld; ORLM's execution remains unavailable. Agreement never certifies formulation equivalence.

The 217 relationships are selected and clustered within 16 problems. Their original, offline-repaired and historically regenerated outputs remain separate. No independent human semantic reference is claimed. D1 remains a completed author-validated, language-model-assisted construction of 12 pairs, without a completed provider-performance experiment. G1–G3 remain feasibility/identifiability audits. Historical negative, inconclusive and unattempted investigations retain their original qualifications and destinations.
