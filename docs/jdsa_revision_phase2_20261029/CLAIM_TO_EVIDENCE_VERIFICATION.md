## Qualitative claims and consistency

- Readiness and full scalar agreement use different predicates; full success implies the readiness gates, but readiness does not imply correct values. The Discussion states this logical distinction without a new causal estimate.
- Matched grounding procedures differ in candidate construction, scoring, context, abstention and decoding. Their gains are not isolated search effects.
- Numerical extraction, structural conformity, solver completion, objective proximity, relationship-label agreement and human semantic correctness remain separate endpoints.
- The common-18 methods are cited to their original publications; local adaptations and results are attributed to the retained records. EquivaMap supports the stronger formulation-equivalence target; the binding preprint supports the structure/binding distinction. See [reference verification](REFERENCE_VERIFICATION.md).
- Abstract remains verbatim (227 whitespace-delimited words); six keywords remain. Introduction changes only its roadmap and still matches the expanded narrative. The complete core Results text, Tables 1–7, algorithm, figure and definitions are preserved.
- Exact repeated paragraphs of at least 45 words were checked within each expanded document: none. A manual thematic review consolidated repeated recovery history and assistance caveats while retaining metric-defining qualifications. Main/supplement overlap that supplies a self-contained scientific definition is intentional.
- Every displayed equation is numbered; citations remain numbered/bracketed. The final two-document build resolves all labels, citations and supplementary references and rejects overfull boxes.
