# Remaining scientific weaknesses and recommendations

These are retained evidence limits, not a request to repair measurements or start new experiments during Stage 2. The immutable [Stage 1 limitations](../../reproducibility/jdsa-v2.0.0/LIMITATIONS.md) remain authoritative.

1. **Restricted task and reference assistance.** The catalog is benchmark-derived and schema identification occurs in a known catalog. Gold-derived key eligibility/other reference inputs differ by experiment. Exposure and duplicated/related templates limit generalization; there is no autonomous formulation claim.
2. **Unavailable original records.** Original headline scalar assignments; complete 60/269/20 subset memberships/outcomes; complete authenticated historical higher-rank lists; full rendered prompts/serving defaults for older scalar LLM arms; complete BGE hash-seed/device/environment; ambiguous full-precision PaMOP objectives; and corrected per-instance OptMATH bootstrap records were not recovered. Newer outputs cannot substitute for them.
3. **Reanalysis is narrower than reproduction.** The curated v2 metrics and projections support specified arithmetic. They do not reconstruct original acquisition, inference, every raw validation check or complete local M5. The historical HF v1.2 package is distinct from both v2 and M5.
4. **Scalar agreement is not semantic correctness.** Equal numeric values can refer to different occurrences. Historical tolerance can accept materially different proportions. Reference-correction overlays remain sensitivity checks, not new predictions or independently validated semantic truth.
5. **Dependence and selected populations.** The original 331 rows have 327 frozen text groups; medium-overlap analyses have only four queries. Controls use 49 selected queries/277 slots. Conditional readiness populations vary by method. The new fractions are descriptive and should not receive naive independent-binomial uncertainty.
6. **Limited causal identification.** Six-method comparisons change multiple components. The frozen-score assignment study identifies decoder behavior only within its retained candidates/scores. Absence of whole-instance significance does not prove equivalence; global-search benefits cannot be inferred for the broad task.
7. **Relationship judgments lack independent reference labels.** Recovery and complete-case selection alter the observed population. Inter-model agreement, valid JSON, and unanimous judgments do not establish correctness. The planned independent 36-probability/12-target review is incomplete and cannot be reported as an outcome.
8. **D1 is an assisted construction.** Twelve pairs and 24 fixtures illustrate semantic differences. Author/AI assistance, reuse and review history prevent treating them as independent blinded gold or provider-performance observations.
9. **Common-18 is not a fair leaderboard.** Prompt/model/solver access differs; source validation is incomplete in public projections. PaMOP aggregate objective-agreement remains withheld. Numeric objective agreement is not formulation equivalence.
10. **Historical auxiliary studies are bounded.** Subset manifests/row outcomes are missing; G1–G3 and unexecuted studies are feasibility/status findings, not zero-accuracy experiments. Earlier baselines and archived scalar LLM arms remain distinct from headline measurements.
11. **Redistribution limits persist.** Gated NLP4LP text/context/value traces, license-unclear OptMATH source/programs, raw provider prose/account metadata, private author conversations and third-party weights remain excluded. Account-specific provider permissions are unverified. No legal inference that all derived data are automatically redistributable is made.
12. **External URL verification is incomplete.** Some publisher/DOI sites rejected automated access or timed out. Canonical ACL alternatives were checked. The link ledger records these limits; access failure is not evidence that a reference is invalid.

A later editorial stage may safely proceed on this bounded scientific framing after author review. It should preserve these limitations, the versioned crosswalk and both frozen statistical families. Stronger unseen-schema, autonomous-formulation, semantic-accuracy or provider-ranking claims would require separately designed and authorized evidence. No such study or Stage 3 was started here.
