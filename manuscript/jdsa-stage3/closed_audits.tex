Its saved slot counts sum to 1705, whereas strict metadata scalars give 1675, differing by 30 slots in seven cases. These baseline definitions cannot be interchanged. The changed cases add 134 occurrences: 132 keys are unannotated in the query reference, and two have symbolic string values. Their correctness is unknown, not automatically false.
