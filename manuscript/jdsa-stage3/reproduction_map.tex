The four headline metric CSVs match their first committed versions, whose runners serialized 13 metric columns and omitted scalar mappings. Integrity therefore supports aggregation, not recovery or corrected-reference reevaluation of original assignments. Saved top-1 IDs support retrieval rates; full historical ranked lists and per-condition sanitization predictions were not recovered. The corrected TF--IDF variant mean remains $(301+299+258)/(3\cdot331)=0.8640$ before rounding.

The Stage-A cache contains 1,705 rows/311 queries; its separate full-frame reference sensitivity retains all 331 queries/1,847 scalars, counting missing selections as missing. The initial factorial collection comprises 49 primary, 12 development, one overlap-reconciliation and two reference-only cases. Local validation checked all 1,176 primary method/query choices against saved candidate values/type rules without rerunning a decoder. Public projections retain scores, indices and metric counts, omitting candidate text, values and source slot names. They permit arithmetic and consistency checks, not renewed source-dependent correctness validation.

The released original metric paths retain the source directory names: \path{results/selective_grounding_rerank/}, \path{results/final_resubmission_method/}, \path{results/dense_retrieval_bge_m3/}, \path{results/oracle_recomputation_2026-08-15/} and \path{results/stage6_matched_grounding_baselines_2026-08-27/}. Public common-18 numeric tokens/dispositions and relationship labels/validity flags support reanalysis of their reported summaries. Raw generated programs, stdout, prompts, full provider responses and source quotations remain excluded. The original M3 evaluator \path{tools/reaggregate_common18_m3.py} and M5 raw-response validator \path{tools/verify_llm217_m5.py} require separately authorized original layouts; the default public verifier does not pretend those inputs exist. D1 publishes original project fixtures and final assisted-author decisions, excluding private review conversations.

The 60/269/20 studies lack full original manifests/outcomes. Five reported examples from each of 60 and 269 include three shared IDs, but cannot reconstruct either population. The 20-case writer also omitted scalar maps. BGE's literal reporting metadata does not authenticate its executed weights, device, environment or hash seed. Historical rendered prompts/provider defaults are incomplete; PaMOP full precision and corrected OptMATH per-instance bootstrap inputs are missing. Unmatched loose blobs, later caches and replay configurations do not fill these gaps.

\subsection{Public Verification and Access Restrictions}
Extract the named evidence ZIP or its versioned HF directory. In a clean Python 3.12 environment, install \path{requirements-validation.txt} (NumPy 2.3.5), then run \texttt{python -I verify.py --output /tmp/jdsa-verification.json}. Outputs stay outside the immutable package. The manifest checks all 172 payload files; the machine-readable report separates supported arithmetic from historical and semantic limitations. Both independently downloaded releases passed 9,737 checks, with six integrity/statistics tests. File/network tracing found no private-path access or network calls during reanalysis; no new inference or scientific solver experiment was performed.

Author-owned evidence uses CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and software MIT without relicensing upstream content. NLP4LP has gated access and CC BY-NC 4.0 metadata; each user must obtain it under applicable terms. OptMATH source licensing and account-specific provider raw-output redistribution rights remain unresolved. NL4Opt source retains its MIT license but its raw corpus is not bundled here. Complete M5 originals, source witnesses and private author reviews remain local. These exclusions bound public verification even where local raw-record checks succeeded.
