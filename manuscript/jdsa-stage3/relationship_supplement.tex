For $N=197$ complete items and $n=3$ raters, let $n_{ic}$ count judgments in nominal category $c$. We recompute
\begin{equation}
\bar P=\frac{1}{N}\sum_i\frac{\sum_c n_{ic}(n_{ic}-1)}{n(n-1)},\quad
P_e=\sum_c\left(\frac{\sum_i n_{ic}}{Nn}\right)^2,\quad
\kappa=\frac{\bar P-P_e}{1-P_e}.
\label{eq:fleiss}
\end{equation}
Using all four categories in Eq.~\eqref{eq:fleiss} gives $\bar P=0.7394247039$, $P_e=0.5858578050$ and $\kappa=0.3708071786$. Complete-case selection is conditional on output validity and may be informative. The residual 20 items are all final Cohere nonliteral-evidence failures, individually listed in the machine-readable \path{llm217_missing20.csv}.
