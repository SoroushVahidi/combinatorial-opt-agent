\section{External Extraction and Semantic Contrast Details}
\subsection{OptMATH Numeric Extraction}
\label{app:extraction-details}\label{subsec:exp_external_validation}
As a component-level check, we evaluated numeric extraction on a fixed 1,000-instance \textsc{OptMATH}-Train sample \cite{lu2025optmath} (no fixed schema catalog, so extraction only).

Pooled raw AST code-value overlap recall is $0.7501$; pooled automatic text-support recall is $0.8118$ (complete-instance rate $0.5910$). The saved automatic category counts classify $2{,}310/31{,}736=7.28\%$ of code-side values across the 1,000 instances as implementation, solver/API, index/loop or array/dimension constants. This is distinct from the 150-instance classifier comparison (seed 20260815). 

The later verification summary reports audit-calibrated text-supported recall as the pooled (micro) ratio $23{,}241/28{,}799=0.8070$ (95\% instance-bootstrap CI $[0.7842,0.8283]$, $B=10{,}000$, seed 42, over the 1,000 sampled instances). Its corrected per-instance counts are unavailable for independent rebootstrap of that historical CI. The reported mean of per-instance ratios (macro recall) is $0.8135$. The stored verification JSON reverses the names of these two aggregates; the values are preserved here with their implemented definitions. The 28,799 values are not independent samples, and occurrence-aware recall is not identifiable from these set-based artifacts. 

Two independently coded classifiers agree on $98.04\%$ of 4,801 literals in the 150-instance sample---automated cross-validation, not human gold labels. This is limited evidence that extraction transfers beyond \textsc{NLP4LP}; it does not establish full-framework generalization.
